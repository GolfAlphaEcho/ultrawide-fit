# Privacy Policy - Ultrawide Fit for YouTube

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Ultrawide Fit for YouTube does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal or user data.

The extension only changes how the YouTube video player is displayed. Your chosen display mode (Off, Zoom or Stretch) is saved using Chrome's built-in storage so it is remembered between sessions. This setting stays in your browser (and, if you use Chrome Sync, your Google account's synced settings) and is never sent to the developer or any third party.

The extension makes no network requests, uses no analytics or tracking, and does not read the content of the pages you visit.

Questions: https://github.com/GolfAlphaEcho/ultrawide-fit/issues
